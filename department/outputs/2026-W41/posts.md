## Post 1: Founder's memory

```
Your best SDR is the founder's memory.

That's the problem.

The founder knows who's warm. Who commented twice last week. Who asked about pricing in March. Who just hired a VP of Sales.

None of it is written down. So nobody else can act on it.

Founder-led sales isn't the bottleneck. Founder-only signal reading is.

Here's how I get it out of their head in one afternoon:

1️⃣ The warm list

Everyone they'd call today if they had the time. Name, company, why.
↳ Usually a few dozen names. Most exist in the CRM as an email address and nothing else.

2️⃣ The trigger list

What made the last 10 closed deals buy when they did. Funding, a new hire, a failed agency, a board target.
↳ This becomes your scoring rubric.

3️⃣ The "why us" notes

The exact sentence each buyer used when they said yes.
↳ This becomes your messaging.

Then put all three where a second person can see them and act on them every week.

When I reactivated 1,200+ dormant accounts with automated sequences, the contacts had been in the CRM all along. What was missing was a reason to talk to them.

P.S: Founders, how many of your warm deals would survive you taking 2 weeks off?
```

**Alt hooks**
- Dialogue: "CEO: 'We need more leads.' Me: 'You have 40 warm ones. They live in your head.'"
- Reframe: "Founder-led sales doesn't break at 10 customers. It breaks the first week the founder gets sick."

**First comment:** "If you want the template I use for the trigger list, reply here and I'll share it."

**Needs from you:** confirm the 1,200+ line matches how that reactivation
actually went. If the gap was something other than context, tell me and I'll
rewrite that line.

## Post 2: Score fit before intent (keyword SCORE)

```
Someone viewed your profile 4 times this week.

Don't message them yet.

High intent from the wrong buyer is a fan, not a lead. Most LinkedIn outreach goes wrong right here.

I score every warm person on two things before I write a word:

1️⃣ Fit (0 to 50)

Title, company size, industry, a visible GTM gap, geography.
↳ Scored once. It doesn't change because they liked a post.

2️⃣ Intent (0 to 50)

Profile view 5. Reaction 3. Comment 12. Comment with a question 18. Inbound DM 25. Funding or a new sales leader +10.
↳ Re-scored weekly. Signals older than 14 days count half. Older than 30 count zero.

3️⃣ One rule on top

Fit under 25 never gets a message. However engaged they are.

The total decides the move:
↳ 75+: research them and reach out within 24 hours
↳ 55 to 74: engage on their posts twice, then reach out
↳ 35 to 54: watch
↳ Below that: leave them alone

Back to the profile viewer.

A founder of a 60-person B2B SaaS company who's hiring a first SDR scores 80. Message today.

A student who viewed 4 times scores 20. Leave it.

Same behaviour. Opposite move.

Comment SCORE and I'll send you the full scoring sheet with every weight.
```

**Alt hooks**
- Stat shock: "A profile view is worth 5 points. A comment with a question is worth 18. Here's the rest of the sheet."
- Contrarian: "The warmest-looking leads in your notifications are usually the worst ones to message."

**First comment:** "The sheet is a Notion page. Duplicate it, change the weights to match your ICP, and score your last 20 engagers this afternoon."

**After posting:** every SCORE commenter goes into Notion Prospects with
+12 intent (keyword comment). Reply in the comments within 2 hours, then DM the
link with one question: "What's the role you most want to see in your notifications?"

**Needs from you:** publish the scoring sheet page in Notion (Share →
Publish) and paste the public link into `proof-bank.md`.
