# ICP v1 (hypothesis, validate with 5 conversations)

Status: draft from play 01, 2026-W40. Confirm or kill each assumption by
end of W43 using the validation questions at the bottom.

## Primary: the founder still closing every deal

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Company | B2B SaaS or tech-enabled services |
| Size | 15 to 150 employees |
| Revenue | roughly $1M to $15M ARR |
| Stage | Seed to Series B, or bootstrapped and profitable |
| Geo | India, US, UK, Singapore, UAE, Australia |
| Buyer titles | Founder, CEO, Co-founder, CRO |
| GTM motion | Founder-led sales or 1 to 3 AEs, no real demand engine |

**What's on their mind right now**
- "Referrals got us here. They won't get us to the next revenue target."
- "I'm the bottleneck in every sales conversation."
- "We tried an agency or SDRs and got meetings that went nowhere."
- "Everyone's talking about AI for GTM and I don't know what's real."

**Trigger events (raise intent score)**
- Raised a round in the last 120 days
- Hiring first SDR, first marketer or Head of Growth
- New CRO or VP Sales in seat < 90 days
- Founder posting about pipeline, hiring sales or "growth" problems

## Secondary: the first marketing or growth leader

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| Company | Same as primary, 40 to 300 employees |
| Buyer titles | Head of Growth, Head of Marketing, VP Marketing, Demand Gen Lead |
| Situation | First or second marketing hire, owns a pipeline number |

**What's on their mind**
- "I own a pipeline target with half a team and no ops support."
- "Leadership wants AI in marketing. I need a plan by next quarter."
- "Sales says my leads are bad."

## Disqualifiers (score fit as 0, don't approach)

- Agencies selling the same services (they're peers, nurture as network)
- B2C, ecommerce, consumer apps
- Enterprises over 1,000 employees (long cycles, procurement-led)
- Students, job seekers, tool vendors pitching us
- No budget signal at all: pre-revenue, solo founders with no team

## Validation questions (ask in the first 5 calls)

1. What's your current main source of pipeline, as a percentage?
2. What have you already tried that didn't work?
3. Who owns the pipeline number today?
4. If this was fixed in 90 days, what would it be worth?
5. What made you reply to my message?
