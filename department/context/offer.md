# Offer

## What Murugan sells

Consulting for B2B companies whose pipeline depends too much on founder
hustle, referrals or one channel. Three entry points:

1. **GTM strategy sprint** (2 to 4 weeks): ICP, positioning, channel mix and a
   90-day pipeline plan with numbers attached.
2. **Demand gen system build**: LinkedIn, outbound and content engines wired
   into one pipeline with signals, scoring and follow-up.
3. **AI and automation in marketing**: replacing manual research, enrichment,
   content and reporting work with Claude-based workflows.

Public framing: **Fractional CMO** (as on the profile), with the GTM strategy
sprint as the default entry offer.

and what I'd fix first. Useful whether or not we work together."
**Who it's for:** prospects scoring 55+ who have replied at least once.
**Booking link:** https://calendly.com/murugan-mga/30min

## Why someone buys

- Pipeline is flat or lumpy and the founder is still the main closer
- They hired SDRs or an agency and the meetings aren't converting
- Leadership asked "what's our AI plan for marketing?" and nobody owns it
- New funding or a new revenue target with no clear channel plan

## Why someone doesn't buy (objections to mine for content)

- "We tried LinkedIn, it doesn't work for us"
- "We need leads now, not strategy"
- "We'll hire a Head of Growth instead"
- "AI content all sounds the same"
- "Our market is too niche for demand gen"
