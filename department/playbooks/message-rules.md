# Message rules (plays 07 and 08)

## The 3-touch path

| Day | Touch | Job | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Engage | Like + thoughtful comment on their recent post | 1 to 3 lines |
| 1 | Opener | Reference the trigger, ask one easy question | < 300 chars |
| 4 | Value | Share one useful thing tied to their situation (asset, stat, teardown) | < 500 chars |
| 9 | Question | A sharper question with an easy out | < 250 chars |

Then stop. Move to "nurture": engage with their content monthly, re-score weekly.
Restart only on a new signal.

## Opener formula

`[What I noticed] + [why it caught my eye] + [one question they can answer in 10 seconds]`

Good:
> Saw you're hiring your first two SDRs at Northwind. Curious, are they going
> to be working inbound from content or cold lists from day one?

Bad:
> Hi Priya, I came across your profile and was impressed by your journey. I help
> B2B companies scale pipeline with AI. Open to a quick call?

## Never

- Pitch, attach a calendar link, or mention price before they reply
- Use "saw your profile", "hope you're well", "quick question", "touching base"
- Send the same sentence twice to the same person
- Send more than 3 unanswered touches
- Use em dashes

## Reply classes (play 08)

| Class | Example | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Interested | "Yes, this is exactly our problem" | Offer Pipeline Teardown + link |
| Curious | Answers the question, no buying signal | Continue conversation, one more question, then offer teardown |
| Not now | "Try me in January" | Set next touch date to that month, log reason |
| Referral | "Talk to our Head of Growth" | Thank, ask for intro or reference them by name |
| Not a fit | "We're B2C" | Thank, close, update fit |
| Unsubscribe | "Not interested" | Close, never touch again |
