# Play 07: Conversation designer

Rules: `department/playbooks/message-rules.md`. Voice: `voice.md`.

## Inputs
The prospect brief from play 06 and their signal log.

## Build the path
1. **Pick the entry** based on the strongest signal:
   - Commented with a question → answer it properly in the comments, then DM
     "Wanted to give you a fuller answer than a comment allows..."
   - Profile view only → engage on their post first, opener 2 days later
   - Keyword comment → send the asset with one question about their situation
   - Trigger event → open on the trigger
2. **Write the full path**: Day 0 comment, Day 1 opener, Day 4 value,
   Day 9 question. Each must reference something different.
3. **Pre-write two branches** for the likely replies (Interested, Curious).
4. **Check**: under length limits, no pitch before reply, no banned phrases,
   no em dashes, nothing that isn't in the brief or proof bank.

## Output
Messages ready to copy, plus Notion Touches rows with status `Drafted` and
due dates. Murugan sends and flips status to `Sent`.
