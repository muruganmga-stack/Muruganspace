# Play 03: Idea miner

## Sources, in priority order
1. `department/inputs/calls/`: call notes, DMs, comments that asked questions
2. Objections listed in `offer.md`
3. Replies and comments logged in Notion (exact phrasing matters)
4. Market shifts: use web search for this week's B2B GTM, demand gen and AI
   marketing news. Look for things that change how a founder should act.
5. Last week's `review.md`: what topics produced ICP comments

## Each idea must have
| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Insight | One sentence, a point of view someone could disagree with |
| Source | Where it came from (call, objection, news link) |
| Pillar | Growth systems / Demand gen & pipeline / AI & automation |
| Job | Familiarity, Curiosity, or Conversation |
| ICP pain | Which line in `icp.md` it touches |
| Proof available | Yes/no from `proof-bank.md` |

## Filter
Kill any idea that: has no point of view, could be posted unchanged by 100
other people, or touches no ICP pain. Output 15, ranked, top 4 starred.
Write to `department/outputs/<week>/ideas.md` and add the top 10 to Notion
Content with status `Idea`.
