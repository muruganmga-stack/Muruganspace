# Play 06: Prospect researcher

## Sources
- Their LinkedIn headline, recent posts and comments (Murugan can paste)
- Apollo / Clay: company size, funding, tech stack, job postings, headcount
  growth. Report credit cost first.
- Web search: company news in the last 90 days
- Notion: their signal log with us

## One-page brief (append to the prospect's Notion page)
| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Snapshot | Title, company, size, stage, geo |
| How they found us | Signals in order, with the post they engaged on |
| What they're probably solving | 2 hypotheses tied to `icp.md`, with evidence |
| Trigger | The single best reason to reach out now |
| Common ground | Shared connections, topics, places, prior interactions |
| Angle | The one question that would make them want to reply |
| Risk | Why this might not be a fit |

Never guess at personal details. If evidence is thin, say "low confidence"
and recommend engaging with their content first instead of messaging.
