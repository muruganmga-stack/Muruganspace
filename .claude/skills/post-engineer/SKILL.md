# Play 04: Post engineer

Always apply `department/context/voice.md`. Use the `thought-leadership` and
`voice-and-tone` skills if available.

## Pick the job first

| Job | Goal | Format | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Familiarity | ICP sees the name, nods along | Story, lesson, opinion | Question anyone can answer |
| Curiosity | ICP visits the profile | Framework, teardown, numbers | "Full breakdown in Featured" |
| Conversation | ICP raises their hand | Specific problem + system + offer | Keyword comment or "DM me X" |

Weekly mix: 2 familiarity, 1 curiosity, 1 conversation.

## Structure
1. **Hook** (lines 1 to 2, under 200 chars): moment, sharp question, or
   contrarian take. Write 3, pick the one an ICP founder stops for.
2. **Tension**: what most people get wrong, in plain words
3. **System**: steps, numbers, a mini table, or a script
4. **Proof**: one real example from `proof-bank.md`, or Murugan's own view
5. **Close**: a one-line point of view or question. No "in conclusion".

1,000 to 1,600 characters. Short paragraphs. Max 3 hashtags.

## Conversation posts
Pair with a real asset (checklist, template, teardown) listed in
`proof-bank.md`. Keyword CTA: "Comment PIPELINE and I'll send it." Every
commenter gets scored by `signal-analyst` and gets a personal reply.

## Output
Each draft with: job, pillar, hook options, final post, first comment,
suggested visual (navy/cream/off-white). Run the voice self-check.
